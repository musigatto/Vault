# Administrative Security Measures (§2.5)

## Staff hiring & leaving process
Personnel security measures from **selection/hiring of staff or contractors → relieving them of duties**:
- **Hiring**: thorough **identity verification + background check** · contractors hired with **same due diligence** as employees · provide orientation sessions (company background, roles & responsibilities, security policies)
- **Contractors**: insert clauses in contract to enforce personnel security; **audit their compliance**
- **Leaving**:
  - Remove access rights; collect all company assets (keys, access cards, systems)
  - Remove critical org data from employee's **personal device** before departure
  - Change network + workstation passwords
  - **Deactivate email accounts + remote-access accounts**
  - Debriefing session — detect unnoticed encrypted files/work-in-progress
  - Remove **biometric / badge access codes**

## Employee monitoring
- Conduct **indiscriminate monitoring of employee activities** to detect policy violations
- Purpose: measure/enhance productivity · secure corporate resources · track attendance + proof of hours
- Prevents/curtails unacceptable behavior before business impact
- Tool: **Spytech SpyAgent** (spy software) — records all user activity:
  - Keystroke logging · screenshot capturing · website activity · application-usage monitoring · email send/receive monitoring · event-timeline logging · social network & chat conversations · webmail/web content · computer-usage logging
  - Sound-triggered microphone recording · webcam capture · files uploaded/downloaded/accessed/printed · window & mouse activity · clipboard logging · activity logging
  - Real-time **remote desktop viewing & control** · program logging · SpyAnywhere cloud access + real-time log viewing · email log delivery · FTP log delivery
