# Network Defense: Goal, Benefits, Challenges (§1.11)

## Goal
- Protect information, systems, network infrastructure from unauthorized access, misuse, modification, service denial, degradation, disruption
- Seek **continual + defense-in-depth security**: predicting, protecting, monitoring, analyzing, detecting, responding
- Rely on **Information Assurance (IA) principles**

## IA principles
| Principle | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Confidentiality | Info not disclosed to unauthorized parties; only authorized users access/use/copy |
| Integrity | Info not modified/tampered/deleted/corrupted without authorization |
| Availability | Info available to authorized parties without disruption |
| Non-repudiation | A party cannot deny sending the message; validates digital signature integrity/origin |
| Authentication | Identity verified via credentials against authorized-user database |

## Benefits
- Slide list: protect information assets · comply with government/industry regulations · ensure secure communication with clients & suppliers · reduce attack risk · gain competitive edge (more secure services)
- Prose: increased profits (prevent loss) · improved productivity (block unproductive browsing/gaming/gossip) · enhanced compliance (real-time monitoring avoids penalties) · client confidence

## Challenges
- Broad: **distributed computing environments** (vast, complex networks) · **emerging threats** (more sophisticated, better organized each day) · **lack of network security skills**
- Network defender challenges: protect from Internet attacks · protect public servers (web, e-mail, DNS) · contain damage on compromise · prevent internal attacks · protect sensitive info (customer DBs, financial records, trade secrets) · develop secure-handling guidelines · enable intrusion detection & logging
